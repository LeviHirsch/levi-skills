# Structure & modules (v0.1)

## Body (sacred — do not redesign)

1. **Purpose & Scope** — Context/Problem · Core Question  
2. **Brief** — thesis answering the Core Question (~1 paragraph)  
3. **Exploration** — Landscape [What] · Principles [Why] · Mechanisms [How]  
4. **Application** — Strategic Vision · Operative Objectives · Immediate Steps  
5. **Conclusion** — Synthesis · Extending Questions  
6. **Sources** (optional) — short non-academic list  

Footnotes OK in body for flexible cites/URLs/internal pointers.
